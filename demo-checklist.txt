FINAL ORGANIZER DEMONSTRATION

1. Main page:
   sudo systemctl status nginx
   curl http://10.10.145.207

2. Second computer:
   From another physical computer on the same network:
   curl http://10.10.145.207

3. Remote modification:
   Modify /var/www/html/index.html from the second computer.
   Then:
   curl http://10.10.145.207
   Show the changed text.

4. Second port:
   curl http://10.10.145.207:8002

5. HTTPS:
   curl -k https://10.10.145.207
   Optionally open https://10.10.145.207 in a browser.
